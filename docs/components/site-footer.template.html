The numbers on this site are derived from the REEport financial report submitted to the National Institute of Food and Agriculture (NIFA) and represent expenses from the most recent federal fiscal year. The report is annual and details how NIFA funding is leveraged with resources from other federal agencies and non-federal entities, such as state appropriations, self-generated funds, industry grants/agreements, gifts and endowments to support the research and outreach of the UC Davis Agricultural Experiment Station.
